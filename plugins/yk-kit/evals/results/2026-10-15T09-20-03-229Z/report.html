**Out of scope [A]**

- **Spreadsheet formula injection:** if a cell starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`, prefix it with `'`. Without this, a spreadsheet could run text from an order as a formula.

1. The button appears on the orders report page for permitted users only.

4. Dates and currency use a consistent, documented format.
5. A large dataset (agree a size, e.g. 50k rows) exports without timing out.
6. A user can't export orders they couldn't see in the report.
7. Cells starting with formula characters are neutralised.
