  - evidence: `(curl|wget)\b[^|]*\|\s*(sudo\s+)?(bash|sh|…)` — probe allowed `/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL …/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"`, `sh -c "$(curl …)"`, `bash <(curl …)`, `eval "$(curl …)"`; and line 45 `if SHELL_RECEIVER.search(head)` only looks before `<<`, so `cat <<'EOF' | bash` with body `sudo rm -rf /x` is allowed although line 5 claims 'a heredoc fed to bash/sh is still checked'.
